Vehicle Accident Cover Procedure

If Comprehensive you :
1.Report to traffic police,Take photos of scene,Notify the insurance company,Take car to garage/assessment centre
2.Submit documents to insurance (Police abstract,Filled claim form,Copy of Drivers id and copy of driving license,Photos)
3.Insurance advice (Assessment report,Required exesss,Investigation when necessary,Accept or Reject claim)
4.If Repairs(Repair authority,Repairs,Reinspection,Vehicle Release Letter)
5.If write off (Salvage taken by insurance,Receive offer to retain salvage,Accept discharge voucher,Transfer vehicle ownership to insurance,Surrender original logbook,Receive cash)

Policy Types

Understanding Your Cover

01
Third Party Only (TPO)
The basic, mandatory cover every motorist must have. Provides cover for third parties in case of bodily injury or property damage. Compensates you against legal liabilities from use of the motor vehicle — including Third Party Injuries and Third Party Property Damage.
02
Comprehensive Insurance
Full protection for you, your car, and third parties against all possible risks. Includes everything in Third Party plus damage to your own vehicle from accidents, fire, theft, and more. Best value for vehicles valued over KShs. 500,000.
Claims Process

Vehicle Accident Cover Procedure

Follow these steps after an accident for a smooth claims process.

1
Report & Document
Report to traffic police, take photos of the scene, notify the insurance company, take car to a garage or assessment centre.
2
Submit Documents
Submit police abstract, filled claim form, copy of driver's ID and driving license, and photos to the insurer.
3
Insurance Review
Insurer provides assessment report, required excess, and investigates when necessary before accepting or rejecting the claim.
4
Resolution
Repairs: authority issued, car repaired, reinspected and released. Write-off: salvage taken, offer made, cash settlement received.
